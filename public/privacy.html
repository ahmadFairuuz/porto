Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

This is a personal portfolio site. I keep data collection to an absolute minimum.

What I collect

The only personal data I receive is what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email address, and message. Form submissions are delivered by EmailJS and arrive in my inbox.

How I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry.
  • Nothing else. I do not sell, rent, or share your details with third parties.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not set tracking cookies and does not run third-party analytics. Fonts are loaded from Fontshare and Google Fonts, which may log the request per their own policies.

Retention

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask me to show or delete any message you have sent at any time by emailing me.

Contact

For any privacy question, reach me through the contact form on the home page.
